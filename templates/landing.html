FAQ
Questions we get asked
all the time
How does the 30-day free trial work?▼
Sign up and get full, unrestricted access for 30 days — completely free, no credit card needed. After 30 days, your account is paused until you choose a plan. All your data is kept safe.
How do I pay for a subscription?▼
Contact us on 061 144 0669 or info@stanleybytes.co.za. We arrange payment via EFT — once paid, we send you a license code to enter in the app and your account activates immediately. No waiting.
Can I use SB Invoices on my phone?▼
Yes — SB Invoices is built mobile-first. It works perfectly on Android and iPhone browsers without any app download. Just open the link, sign in, and start creating documents.
What types of documents can I create?▼
Invoices, Receipts, Quotations, and Damage / Service Reports. All generate professional PDFs with your business branding and banking details applied automatically.
Can I add my logo and banking details?▼
Absolutely. In Settings, add your business logo, banking details, and default terms. They appear on every PDF automatically — you never have to add them manually.
Who is SB Invoices built for?▼
Anyone who needs to create business documents — freelancers, contractors, tradespeople, small business owners, service providers, consultants, and more. If you invoice clients, SB Invoices is for you.
How do I share invoices with customers?▼
After creating a document, tap "Share PDF". You can share via WhatsApp, email, download it, or open it in your browser. Works seamlessly on all mobile devices.
