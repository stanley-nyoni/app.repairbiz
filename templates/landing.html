FAQ
Frequently asked questions
How does the 30-day free trial work?▼
Sign up and get full access for 30 days — completely free, no credit card needed. After 30 days, your account is paused until you subscribe.
How do I pay for a subscription?▼
Contact us on 061 144 0669 or info@stanleybytes.co.za. We arrange payment via EFT. Once paid, we send you a license code to enter in the app and your account activates immediately.
Can I use RepairBiz on my phone?▼
Yes — RepairBiz is built mobile-first. It works perfectly on Android and iPhone browsers. No app download needed. Just open the link and sign in.
What types of documents can I create?▼
Invoices, Receipts, Quotations, and Damage/Service Reports. All generate professional PDFs with your business branding and banking details automatically.
Can I add my logo and banking details?▼
Yes. In Settings, add your business logo, banking details and default terms. They appear on every PDF automatically — you never add them manually each time.
How do I share invoices with customers?▼
After creating a document, tap "Share PDF". You can share via WhatsApp, email, download it, or open it in your browser. Works on all mobile devices.
