Cookie Policy

How SB Invoices uses cookies and local storage

Last updated: March 2026 · Effective immediately

Short version: SB Invoices uses minimal storage — only what's needed to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit. SB Invoices uses a combination of cookies and browser localStorage to function properly.

2. What We Store and Why

SB Invoices stores the following in your browser:

Name Type Purpose Duration
rb_token localStorage Your login session token — keeps you logged in so you don't have to sign in every time 90 days
rb_business localStorage Your business profile data — cached locally to load the dashboard instantly without waiting for a server request Until logout

That's it. No advertising cookies. No analytics cookies. No third-party tracking.

3. Essential Storage Only

Everything we store is strictly necessary for the app to function. Without rb_token you would be logged out every time you close the browser. Without rb_business the dashboard would load slower as it would need to fetch your profile from the server on every page load.

Because we only use essential storage, we do not need to show a cookie consent banner — there is nothing to consent to beyond what is required to use the service.

4. What We Don't Use

SB Invoices does not use:

  • Google Analytics or any analytics tracking
  • Facebook Pixel or any social media tracking
  • Advertising or retargeting cookies
  • Third-party cookies of any kind
  • Fingerprinting or device tracking

5. How to Clear Your Storage

You can clear the data SB Invoices stores at any time by:

  • Clicking Sign Out in the app — this clears both rb_token and rb_business immediately
  • Clearing your browser's localStorage via your browser settings
  • Using your browser's developer tools to manually delete the keys

Clearing this storage will log you out of the app. Your account data on our servers is not affected — you can log back in at any time.

6. Third-Party Services

When you use SB Invoices, requests are made to our servers hosted on Railway or Render. These hosting providers may set their own cookies for load balancing and security purposes. These are infrastructure-level cookies outside our control but are strictly functional.

Emails sent by SB Invoices go through Resend. We have disabled all tracking pixels and click tracking in our email configuration — Resend does not track whether you open our emails or click links in them.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever add new storage items we will update this policy and notify users via email. We are committed to keeping our storage footprint minimal.
