Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Last updated: March 2026 · Effective immediately

SB Invoices is operated by StanleyBytes, a South African software company. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our invoicing platform.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you register for SB Invoices we collect:

  • Your name and business name
  • Email address and password (stored as a secure hash)
  • Phone number and business address

Business Data

To provide the invoicing service we store:

  • Invoices, receipts, quotations and damage reports you create
  • Customer names, phone numbers, email addresses and physical addresses
  • Your banking details for inclusion on invoices
  • Your business logo and any photos uploaded to documents
  • Payment records and expense entries

Usage Information

We automatically collect basic usage data including login timestamps and login count. We do not use third-party analytics tools or tracking pixels.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to provide and improve SB Invoices:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To generate PDF documents and store your business records
  • To send transactional emails — verification codes, welcome messages, license activation confirmations
  • To send trial expiry reminders
  • To provide customer support when you contact us

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not share your data with third parties except as described in Section 4.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. Passwords are hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and are never stored in plain text. All connections to SB Invoices are encrypted via HTTPS.

Your business data — invoices, customer records, banking details — is isolated per account. No other business can access your data.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information but no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate SB Invoices:

  • Resend — for sending transactional emails. Your email address is shared with Resend solely to deliver emails to you.
  • Railway / Render — for hosting our servers and storing your data. Data is stored in South Africa or the nearest available region.
  • Neon — for database hosting. Your data is stored in their secure infrastructure.

We do not use Facebook Pixel, Google Analytics, or any advertising tracking services.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data we hold about your business
  • Request correction of any inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your documents as PDF at any time

To exercise any of these rights contact us at info@stanleybytes.co.za or WhatsApp 061 144 0669.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion we will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

If your subscription expires and your account is not renewed, we retain your data for 90 days before deletion to allow for reactivation.

7. Children's Privacy

SB Invoices is a business tool intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email. Continued use of SB Invoices after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For any privacy-related questions or requests:
